Every refund runs the same four steps

Refund request
  1. 1

    Verify

    Match the order, the amount, and the card on file. A mismatch goes to a person.

  2. 2

    Score

    The fraud model scores the request against the customer’s last ninety days.

  3. 3

    Approve

    Scores under the threshold approve on their own; the rest wait for review.

  4. 4

    Settle

    The processor returns the funds and posts one line to the ledger.
