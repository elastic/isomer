# Definition

The in-repo reference pack. Primitives: `agendaSlide`, `closingSlide`, `quoteSlide`, `sectionSlide`, `slideAnnotatedRender`, `slideBars`, `slideBulletList`, `slideCode`, `slideCodeTrace`, `slideColumns`, `slideCommand`, `slideDefinitions`, `slideDelta`, `slideDiff`, `slideFanout`, `slideFrame`, `slideGraph`, `slideHeading`, `slideLanes`, `slideLayers`, `slideList`, `slideMatrix`, `slidePipeline`, `slideQuadrant`, `slideRender`, `slideRenderGrid`, `slideRoadmap`, `slideSequence`, `slideSource`, `slideSplitLayout`, `slideStackLayout`, `slideStat`, `slideStats`, `slideTable`, `slideTerritoryGroup`, `slideTimeline`, `slideTranscript`, `slideTree`, `slideWindowLayout`, `statementSlide`, `titleSlide`. Surfaces: React, HTML, text, Markdown, Slack, and SVG, the last only when the runtime is given frames. Every primitive has a native `slack` renderer, and every `markdown` renderer returns `md` builder content. `slideSplitLayout` takes its two panes as `SlideSplitLayout.Pane` children in JSX, and each pane's primitives are that pane's children. `slideStackLayout` and `slideWindowLayout` take primitives as children. `slideColumns` takes `SlideColumns.Column` children, and `slideTranscript` takes `SlideTranscript.Turn` children. `slideCommand`'s command is its text, `slideCode` is one panel whose `text`, its source as dedented text children (often a template literal), sits beside `file`, `language`, and `lineNumbers`, `slideCodeTrace` takes two `SlideCodeTrace.Panel` children of the same shape joined by an arrow, and a panel's `lineNumbers` (`true` or `{ start, highlight }`, as `EuiCode` takes them) numbers its lines in a gutter, and `slideDiff`'s text is its source, read as a dedented template literal, with `remove` and `add` range strings counting its lines from 1 and an optional `lineNumbers` gutter numbering the file after the change. `slideStat` and `slideDelta` take their sentence as text, `slideStats` takes `SlideStats.Stat` children, and `slideBars` takes `SlideBars.Bar` children labelled by their text. `slideTable` and `slideMatrix` keep their grids as props, and `slideQuadrant` takes `SlideQuadrant.Cell` children holding `SlideQuadrant.Item`. A diagram's parts are children of their kind: `SlideFanout.Target`, `SlideGraph.Node`, `SlideLanes.Lane` and `SlideLanes.Note`, `SlideLayers.Layer`, `SlidePipeline.Step` and `SlidePipeline.Span`, `SlideSequence.Actor` and `SlideSequence.Message`, `SlideTerritoryGroup.Territory`, `SlideTimeline.Item`, and `SlideTree.Item`; ids and chips stay attributes. `slideGraph` derives its arrows (`graphEdges`): the main row joins left to right, and an above or below node names the main-row node it joins in `of`, its arrow pointing into the row unless `direction: 'out'`. `slideAnnotatedRender` takes its `SlideRender` and `SlideAnnotatedRender.Pin` children side by side, and `slideRenderGrid` takes `SlideRenderGrid.Tile` children. A content primitive's list is children, members of its component named for their role, each child's sentence its text: `slideBulletList` takes `SlideBulletList.Item`, `slideDefinitions` `SlideDefinitions.Definition`, `slideList` `SlideList.Item`, and `slideRoadmap` `SlideRoadmap.Column` holding `SlideRoadmap.Item`. `slideHeading`'s title is its text. Whole-slide layouts end in `Slide`. Containers that arrange primitives inside a slide end in `Layout`. In JSX, `agendaSlide` takes `AgendaSlide.Section` children, `closingSlide` takes `ClosingSlide.Link` and `ClosingSlide.Path`, `sectionSlide` takes `SectionSlide.Item` children in `slides` (each `{ text, href? }`), `quoteSlide`, `statementSlide`, and `slideSource` take their text as children, and `titleSlide` takes `TitleSlide.Definition` and `TitleSlide.Aside`. `slideFrame`'s `footer` is a `SlideFrame.Footer` child; its other element children fill `body`. Nested nodes are always children, a collection of records brands a child component, a primitive's one authored text is its text children, and scalars and enums stay props; `slideTable`, `slideMatrix`, and `slideQuadrant` keep their grids as props. A render's `body` is the slide it embeds, validated by `schemaFor` but not a walked child, so its ids are its own and it renders no node anchors; in JSX it is the render's or grid's other primitive children, through `fromNodeChildren`. The pack registers two enhancements: `slideBuildsEnhancement`, which turns on anchors so a host can reveal an ordered primitive one part per click with `showSlideBuild`: a `slideBulletList` item, a `slideList` row, a `slidePipeline` step, a `slideSequence` message, a `slideTimeline` item, or a `slideTranscript` turn, and `slideCopyEnhancement` (id `SLIDE_COPY`), which adds a Copy button to each `slideCommand` where its script runs. The pack declares `authoring.groups` and exports `buildSlidesAuthoringPrompt`, `slideJsx`, `slidePrimitiveGroups`, and `slideFontFaces`. The pack ships its own Distillate HTML adapter, `createDistillateStyleAdapter`, whose `styleCollector` tag is `DISTILLATE_STYLE_COLLECTOR`.[^pack][^docs]
